## Design Specificity Verdict

Specific in its evidence layer. Category-standard in its page architecture. And its headline world-claim is broken by a cartoon on the flagship.

**For.** The `Reading` component is the product's thesis as a component: a real `<dl>` with `sr-only` `dt`s under a weight-and-style mark, whose datum is `path#L16–L26 @ sha7` as a live commit-pinned permalink. `src/data/proof.ts` is the whole product in 89 lines — permalinks pinned to `blob/<sha>/…`, never a branch — and it parses the quoted bytes as structure rather than re-typing them. The NL2REGEX permalink resolves HTTP 200. Zero radius, zero shadow, zero icon, zero third-party runtime request, held across all four routes. Under forced colours the marks survive as `2px dashed rgb(0,0,0)` — the Weight-Before-Hue Rule holds.

**Against.** Strip the marks and it is a dark two-column portfolio: hero-photo → 4-up strip → alternating rows → about → contact. Every value on all four routes is undatum'd — `ChannelStrip` puts `23.3% → 43.8%`, `172 cases`, `8`, `PyPI` on every route with no datum and no permalink: 16 violations of the system's own Datum Rule (`DESIGN.md:185`). And the flagship's artifact is a cartoon.

**Deterministic scan.** `impeccable detect --json index.html src` → exit 0, 40 findings, all one advisory rule (`design-system-font-size`). Browser overlay on four routes → 77 findings (`tiny-text` ×75, `undersized-ui-text` ×1, `line-length` ×1). Combined 117 raw; after false-positive calls 3 are real, and all three share one root cause (P2-5). The 39 CLI false positives come from a doc-parsing collapse: DESIGN.md's prose documents Label as "10–13px" (a range) and Body as "0.875rem → 0.9375rem", but its frontmatter declares single values `11px`/`14px`. None acknowledged in `config.json` `ignoreValues`.

**Visual overlays.** Injection succeeded — preflight set `document.title` and appended an executing `<script>`; `detect.js` (2.2 MB) was injected on four fresh tabs and the detector ran in-page. Overlay visibility verified by pixels, not intent: 2,760 of 144,000 sampled pixels differ from the plain capture, dominated by the amber `rgb(255,186,0)` highlight boxes. But there is no `[Human]` tab to look at — this harness exposes no browser-presentation mechanism (`impeccable --help` has no `present` verb, no extension relay). Overlays archived at `/tmp/B-overlay-{home,projects,projects-shepherd,experience}.png`. The transient live server on `:8400` was started and stopped (port verified dead).

## Overall Impression

The most honest portfolio I have critiqued, and its honesty is structural rather than decorative — it volunteers its own weakness at the point of maximum claim. It also fails its own thesis on its most important page, and the failure is a cartoon dog. The biggest opportunity is not a redesign: the flagship has a real artifact shipped to `dist/`, referenced nowhere, while a 3.77 MB emoji holds the slot.

## What's Working

1. The evidence layer resolves and survives falsification. `proof.ts` pins every permalink to `blob/<sha>/…` and computes each trace call's actual line number out of the quoted source. NL2REGEX → 200; `heal.a2a.ing` → `307 → /en/landing → 200`.
2. The accessibility floor is real where it is claimed and measurably excellent: across 110 text nodes / 17 colour pairs on `/`, the lowest measured contrast is 7.67:1 with 0 AA and 0 AAA failures. Reduced motion: `document.getAnimations()` returns 0 before and after scroll. CLS = 0. Zero `<img>` missing alt, zero missing width/height, one h1 per route, zero heading-order jumps, skip link first, 15/15 of the first 15 tab stops carry the single documented `--ink` ring.
3. The restraint is total: zero border-radius in `src/`, zero box-shadow, zero icon font, zero third-party runtime request, self-hosted preloaded fonts. Zero uncaught exceptions and zero 404s on all four routes.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] The flagship's artifact is a cartoon emoji — while the real capture ships unused
**What.** `projects.ts:43` sets `image: '/assets/captures/shepherd.png'`. That file is a cartoon German shepherd head emoji on a white slab, 2848×1496, 3,774,019 bytes — 12× every other capture and 55% of `/`'s entire 6.7 MB payload. Meanwhile `public/assets/captures/gh-shepherd.png` (164 kB, a real `Ravicha2/Shepherd` repo capture at `e4d177f`, 217 commits, real issue titles) is shipped to `dist/` and referenced nowhere; three more `gh-*` captures are orphaned (~740 kB).
**Why it matters.** Shepherd is the first record row on `/`, the first channel on every strip, and the default case study — the one place the product's success criterion must land ("confirm the applied-AI systems-engineering claim from the site alone"). Instead it shows a meme and renders zero artifacts (`CaseStudyView.tsx:243` renders `{proof && …}`; Shepherd has no `proof`). `DESIGN.md:118` promises "Real rasters only"; `shepherd.png` is not in its imagery table.
**Fix.** Point `projects.ts:43` at `gh-shepherd.png` with a real provenance caption, delete `shepherd.png`, and add a `proof` of `kind: 'capture'` quoting `benchmark/benchmark.md` at a pinned sha. Keep the "in progress" verdict.
**Command:** `/impeccable polish` on `projects.ts` + `CaseStudyView.tsx`

### [P1] Focus is never painted on any capture frame
**What.** `Capture.tsx:82,92` sets `focus-visible:outline-offset-[-2px]`, overriding the global `accessibility.css:13` `outline-offset: 2px`. Adjudicated with a controlled pixel test: focusing the frame renders 0 differing pixels (all three frames) while the control element renders 1,032. The outline is not painted at all.
**Why it matters.** The capture frame is the primary action — a link on `/`, on all four flagship case studies, and on `/experience`. PRODUCT.md calls accessibility "a hard floor, not an enhancement"; this is a hard WCAG 2.4.7 failure on the site's most-travelled link.
**Fix.** Delete the `outline-offset-[-2px]` override; the ring already clears 14.93:1 on the bench outside the frame.
**Command:** `/impeccable audit` on `Capture.tsx` + `accessibility.css`

### [P1] Shepherd gets three contradictory verdicts on three surfaces
**What.** `BenchEntry.tsx:55` — `const flagship = Boolean(project.proofLine)`. The tier keys off a proofLine string, not a proof artifact. Shepherd has `proof: false` but a `proofLine`, so it is classified flagship, receives `mark-clean` (which `Reading.tsx:11` defines as "Measured · holds"), and is filed under the heading "Measured to an artifact" (`ProjectsView.tsx:92`) — while its case study says "In progress · not yet settled" with no artifact rendered.
**Why it matters.** With the legend removed, a reader cannot learn the mark means tier here and verdict there, so they read Shepherd as settled — the exact misreading "in progress" exists to prevent. It also under-states real evidence: "Repository only" heads P.06 (an IEEE Xplore paper) and P.07 (`heal.a2a.ing`, live, plus an award). Principle 1 breached in both directions.
**Fix.** Rename the tiers to what they assert ("Measured to an artifact" / "Evidence in the repository") and drive them off the same predicate the strip uses.
**Command:** `/impeccable clarify` on `ProjectsView.tsx` + `BenchEntry.tsx`

### [P1] The verdict word is hidden from assistive tech — and set at 10px
**What.** `ChannelStrip.tsx:72` marks the status span `aria-hidden="true"`: the ARIA snapshot is `link "Shepherd 23.3% → 43.8% pooled precision…"` with no "in progress". The strip is the only place on `/`, `/projects` and `/experience` where the flagship verdicts are stated. Separately the detector flagged that verdict's `dd` as `undersized-ui-text` at 10px, below its own 11px floor.
**Why it matters.** The site's most important honesty signal is invisible to assistive tech exactly where it is most visible to everyone else. `DESIGN.md:239` claims "nothing is the only carrier of its meaning"; here the word is the only carrier for an AT user, and it is hidden.
**Fix.** Fold the status into the link's accessible name and keep the visible span `aria-hidden` so nothing is announced twice; raise the verdict text to 11px.
**Command:** `/impeccable audit` on `ChannelStrip.tsx` + `Reading.tsx`

### [P2] The type ramp and DESIGN.md have both drifted
**What.** One root cause, three real detector findings, plus three documentation overclaims. Build: `HomeView.tsx:88` is `text-[25px]`, off-ramp and unacknowledged, while its sibling h2 two sections later (`:242`) is 15px; `/experience` carries a ~162 char/line run against DESIGN.md's own 66ch rule; the strip's `note` fields are full sentences in 11px mono, which the Value-Is-Mono Rule forbids ("Iosevka … never a label or a sentence") — the bulk of the 75 `tiny-text` hits. Doc: `DESIGN.md:98` says "Exactly two authored keyframes exist in the entire build" — six more exist in `index.css:31-91` and the guard test scopes its count to `benchCss` only; `DESIGN.md:273` says `detect --json` returns `[]` — true bare, false for `index.html src` (40); `DESIGN.md:236` claims the `P.NN` ref is echoed in the strip — it is not.
**Why it matters.** The behaviour is almost all fine (the six extra keyframes are view-transition-only, correctly gated and suppressed). The problem is that on a site whose first principle is "evidence beats adjectives", the design document is the one artifact overstating its own guarantees.
**Fix.** Reconcile the type steps and restate the three overclaims as what they are.
**Command:** `/impeccable typeset` on the type ramp, then `/impeccable document` to resync DESIGN.md

## Questions to Consider

1. If Shepherd cannot be settled publicly, why does it hold the position of honour on all four routes? The site says "not yet settled" then gives it first row, first channel, and default case study. Would promoting a settled project make the claim stronger rather than weaker?
2. What is the strip for? It places four undatum'd figures — the one thing the Datum Rule forbids — on every route, and forces the verdict word to be aria-hidden. If it is navigation, name the projects without numbers; if it is evidence, give each number its datum and permalink.
3. The legend was removed because it read as overselling. What replaced it is less checkable and more ambiguous — four bare figures and a rule that means tier in one place and verdict in another. Would one 12px line at the top of `/projects` cost less than the confusion it removes?
4. The room carries no colour of its own — so what is a cartoon doing in it?
5. For a Sydney recruiter the three decisive facts are "graduating Dec 2026", "485 visa eligible", "Sydney based". One is on the site, on desktop only. Where does availability belong if the goal is confirming role fit "without emailing first"?
